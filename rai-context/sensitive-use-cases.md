## Sensitive use cases

### Consequential impact on legal position or life opportunities

The known or reasonably foreseeable use or misuse of the AI application could affect an individual's:

* Legal status, such as whether an individual is recognized as a minor, adult, parent, guardian, or person with a disability, as well as their marital, immigration, and citizenship status
* Legal rights, particularly in the context of the criminal justice system
* Access to credit, education, employment, healthcare, housing, insurance, and social welfare benefits, services, or opportunities, or the terms on which they are provided

Examples of AI applications that could result in consequential impacts for an individual include:

| Example                                                                          | Description of AI application functions                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Criminal justice system risk assessment and scoring systems                      | Pretrial assessment of whether to detain an individual based on inferred likelihood of appearing for court dates, recidivism, or re-arrest, or post-trial recommendations on sentencing, rehabilitation programs, or parole release.             |
| Court case prioritization systems                                                 | Assisting judges and court officials with resource allocation by classifying legal cases or court submissions according to their likelihood of dismissal.                                                                                      |
| Translation systems for immigration hearings                                     | Assisting decision-making in immigration cases, such as providing real-time translation and transcription during immigration case hearings.                                                                                                   |
| Higher education admission systems                                               | Ranking and classifying applicants for admission to higher education institutions.                                                                                                                                                             |
| Classroom engagement systems                                                     | Measuring classroom engagement and participation by students, including methods such as emotion classification.                                                                                                                               |
| Subsidized housing systems                                                       | Prioritizing candidates for government-subsidized housing.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Home appraisal systems                                                           | Estimating home appraisal values using neighborhood demographics.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Credit scoring and application systems                                           | Determining and allocating credit, such as whether credit is available to an individual based on AI-powered identity verification or a prediction about the individual's likelihood to repay a loan.                                            |
| Interest rate recommendation systems                                             | Recommending variable introductory interest rate offers based on the socioeconomic status of a resident's neighborhood or ZIP code.                                                                                                            |
| Health insurance systems                                                         | Partially relying on wearable health tracker data to assist health insurance companies with coverage and premium decisions.                                                                                                                     |
| Candidate recommendation systems for hiring                                      | Analyzing job applicant databases and recommending candidates for interviews.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Monitoring systems used to measure employee productivity or influence workplaces | Monitoring employees at work to measure and improve productivity, nudge behaviors, or monitor workspaces to influence employee behavior.                                                                                                       |

### Risk of physical or psychological injury

The known or reasonably foreseeable use or misuse of the AI application could result in significant physical or psychological injury to an individual.

Examples of AI applications that could result in significant physical or psychological injury include:

| Example                                              | Description of AI application functions                                                                                                                                                         |
|------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Medical diagnostic or treatment systems              | Supporting decisions for medical diagnoses or treatment.                                                                                                                                        |
| Mental health and well-being systems                  | Analyzing user data to predict the risk of self-harm, abuse of others, or other mental health conditions.                                                                                         |
| Worker safety alert systems                          | Detecting risks to workers in safety-critical environments, such as whether workers are wearing protective equipment, are distracted, or face hazards in the work environment.                   |
| Equipment control and predictive maintenance systems | Controlling equipment and predicting maintenance needs in safety-critical environments.                                                                                                          |
| Autonomous driving systems                           | Driving vehicles with varying levels of autonomy.                                                                                                                                                 |

### Threat to human rights

The known or reasonably foreseeable use or misuse of an AI application could restrict, infringe upon, or undermine an individual's ability to realize their human rights. Because human rights are often interrelated, AI applications could affect many internationally recognized human rights. While the other two Sensitive Use categories, consequential impacts and risk of physical or psychological injury, also implicate certain human rights, this Sensitive Use category focuses on the following rights:

* Human dignity and equality in the enjoyment of rights
* Freedom from discrimination
* Life, liberty, and security of a person
* Equal protection of the law and criminal justice systems
* Protection against arbitrary interference with privacy
* Freedom of movement
* Freedom of thought, conscience, and religion
* Freedom of opinion and expression
* Peaceful assembly and association

Examples of AI applications that could present a threat to human rights include:

| Example                                                                           | Description of AI application functions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Social credit-scoring system                                                      | Government determination of a "trustworthiness" or social credit score for citizens based on their behavior, associates, religious or other beliefs and opinions, or what they read.                                                                                           |
| Surveillance systems that could threaten the right to peacefully protest          | AI applications relying on facial recognition or other biometric AI technologies that governments could deploy to surveil individuals at peaceful protests.                                                                                                                    |
| Tracking systems that could be used to restrict freedom of movement               | AI applications used by governments to verify residents' identities and real-time locations without legal justification, which could discourage residents from leaving home or attending certain functions.                                                                    |
| Task-monitoring systems that can interfere with privacy                            | Task-tracking features that could reveal intimate personal information, such as an individual's sexual orientation.                                                                                                                                                            |
| Synthetic media systems that could generate disinformation or political propaganda | Generating synthetic text, video, images, code, or speech (such as a politician's voice) that could disseminate disinformation or political propaganda, threatening human dignity, freedom of thought, freedom of expression, and access to accurate information.                  |
| Alert systems based on religious, political, or social expressions                | Government suppression of religious expression by, for example, flagging individuals wearing certain religious headgear as "suspicious persons."                                                                                                                             |

### Essential infrastructure services

AI applications that control, monitor, or influence the operation of the following essential services:

Critical digital infrastructure includes:

* Provision and operation of internet exchange point services
* Provision of domain name system (DNS) services, excluding services related to root name servers
* Operation and administration of top-level domain (TLD) name registries
* Provision of cloud computing services
* Monitoring environmental or operational safety in data centers
* Provision of content delivery networks (distributed networks of servers that cache web content)
* Provision of trust services (electronic signatures and seals, time stamps, document authentication, website certification, and preservation of electronic data)
* Provision of publicly available electronic communications services (Internet access, interpersonal communications, and transmission services)
* Provision of public electronic communications networks (public telephone, mobile, cable, satellite, and Internet transmission systems)

Additional essential services include:

* Supply of energy
* Supply of water
* Supply of heating
* Supply of electricity
* Supply of gas
* Direction of road traffic
* Transportation, transportation facilities, and transportation systems
* Telecommunications
* Management and operation of nuclear materials
* Other infrastructure whose failure or misuse could cause significant disruption, safety risks, or harm to public welfare

Examples of AI applications that could present essential infrastructure services:

> [!NOTE]
> AI applications used solely for routine cybersecurity purposes, such as threat detection and asset monitoring, are excluded.

| Example                                                   | Description of AI application functions                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Distribution grid optimization service                    | AI-based grid control that forecasts load, dispatches distributed energy resources, and issues automated setpoints to substation equipment, such as switches and capacitor banks.                                                             |
| AI-driven water treatment                                 | A plant-wide controller that tunes coagulant and chlorine dosing based on real-time turbidity and residual measurements and can start or stop high-lift pumps and open or close valves to meet pressure targets.                                |
| AI-enabled telecommunications network optimization        | A self-optimizing 5G RAN service that rewrites base-station configurations to rebalance traffic during major events, including dynamic power control and neighbor-list updates across hundreds of cells.                                        |

### Essential public services

AI applications that influence or determine a community's allocation of resources for the following essential public services and benefits:

* Eligibility and decisions for essential public assistance benefits and services, including healthcare services and decisions to grant, reduce, revoke, or reclaim benefits
* Prioritization and dispatch of emergency first-response services and emergency healthcare triage, such as call classification and dispatch priority for police, fire, EMS, and emergency patient triage
* Public-sector qualification and decision-making for providing public services, such as verifying eligibility for public programs, making allocation decisions, or assessing and collecting public fees
* Operation and allocation within public healthcare delivery and utilization systems, such as organizing or operating access to publicly provided healthcare
* Creditworthiness and credit scoring, including services provided by private entities but excluding fraud detection tools
* Life and health insurance risk assessment and pricing, including services provided by private entities
* Services that affect democratic electoral processes, including voter registration and ballot counting

Examples of AI applications that could present essential public services:

| Example                                    | Description of AI application functions                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|--------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Emergency call screening tool              | Evaluates and triages emergency calls to establish priority when dispatching emergency first-response services.                                                                                                                    |
| Transit optimization engine                | Influences transportation resource allocation, where algorithmic decisions determine which communities receive more or less service, potentially affecting mobility access and equity.                                           |
| School-district planning tool              | Determines education resource allocation, shaping how funding, staffing, and specialized support are distributed across schools or student populations.                                                                           |
| Automated voter-roll validation system     | Affects democratic electoral processes by influencing voter registration accuracy or ballot-handling workflows, where errors could affect eligibility and participation.                                                          |

## Other Sensitive Uses considerations

### Autonomous Agentic AI

All Autonomous Agentic AI Systems that are classified as Computer Use Agents (CUA), multi-agent systems, general-purpose systems, or that fall within existing Sensitive Use categories must undergo the Sensitive Use review process.

For more information about what qualifies as an autonomous agentic AI system requiring this review, and to see examples, review *Agentic Systems in Scope for Sensitive Uses Review*.

General-purpose autonomous agents are designed to perform a potentially limitless range of tasks across different domains, including tasks for which they have not been explicitly trained and actions that may not be anticipated. These systems can adapt to new tasks and environments, often using learning or reasoning capabilities. General-purpose autonomous agents are not limited to a single function; they can do anything they are permitted to do. Their versatility and flexibility also make them more complex to govern, secure, and align with safety protocols.

### Professional mental health services

AI-driven provision of professional mental or behavioral healthcare services to a person. This includes any AI application that:

* Delivers or simulates services typically performed by licensed professionals, such as diagnosis, treatment planning, therapy, or prevention of mental, emotional, or behavioral disorders
* Is presented, explicitly or implicitly, as offering such professional services, for example, by describing itself as a "therapist," "psychiatrist," or "clinical counselor"

General self-help and wellness applications such as mental health education apps, stress trackers, mood journals, and relaxation reminders are not in scope for Sensitive Use review provided they do not claim to diagnose, treat, or mimic therapy.

### Facial recognition technology for law enforcement

Per FR 1.8 of the *Specific Requirements for Facial Recognition* under the Responsible AI Standard, if a law enforcement customer requests our development or deployment assistance with a solution that involves facial recognition technology, regardless of location, that scenario is considered a sensitive use. Teams must report the case to the Office of Responsible. Refer to the *Custom Solutions* guidance for definitions of qualifying development and deployment activities.

Microsoft policy prohibits the use of Microsoft facial recognition technology by or for U.S. state or local police. Customers may not use facial recognition features included in Azure services, such as Face API or Azure Video Indexer, by or for a police department in the United States. Customers found to be violating this policy may have their service suspended. Additional information about this policy is available in the related guidance.

### Emerging Technology Guidance

Emerging Technology Guidance applies to particular use cases, AI applications, or emerging technology scenarios. It provides non-mandatory guidance and best practices that engineering teams can use ahead of formal policy requirements.

These documents serve two purposes:

1. Unblock teams with short-term guidance
2. Provide a foundational resource for developing RAI policies

They can provide general guidance and best practices and serve as the basis for case-specific requirements resulting from a Sensitive Uses review of a model, product, feature, or customer-specific AI solution.

Emerging Technology Guidance topics include:

* Geolocation
* Pronouns and misgendering (for LLM features or systems that infer or refer to pronouns or personal characteristics)
* Statistically rigorous RAI evaluations
* Video

### Technical briefs

Technical briefs provide foundational primers on technical topics important to AI safety, helping readers build a shared understanding of emerging concepts, capabilities, and risks. These documents do not contain policy guidance or requirements for teams building with AI, but they may serve as references for policy, guidance, or review decisions.

Technical brief topics include:

* Chain of thought
* Innovations in open-source architecture
